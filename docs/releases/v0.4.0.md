### New
- **Panels above your character**: the incoming-damage forecast (and the round chart stacked on it) now docks above the player by default, so co-op teammates stay visible. `forecast_dock` in `SpireLedger.conf` moves them left, right or below.
- **Party damage stays next to the names** in the player list; **N** shows/hides it.
- **Player names in the co-op combat log** ("Strike (Kestrel)") instead of character names.

### Fixed
- Card history line reads "25% wins in 4 runs with it" (FR: "25 % de victoires sur 4 parties avec elle"), with correct French singular/plural.
- Forecast labels (HP, Safe, Blk) follow the game language (PV, Aucun risque, Arm.).
- "Badges:" no longer has the French space before the colon in English; the Ledger's per-fight player labels read P1/P2 in English.

### Install
Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831558895) (friends-only for now), or unzip and copy `SpireLedger.dll` and `SpireLedger.json` into the game's `mods` folder.
