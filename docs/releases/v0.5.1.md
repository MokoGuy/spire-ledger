### Fixed
- **Forecast with Tungsten Rod**: it takes 1 off each HP loss, as in the game, instead of being ignored (the forecast showed 2 HP left where you kept 3). Intangible (each loss becomes 1) and Buffer (cancels the next losses) are handled the same way.
- **Boxes under offered cards**:
  - **I** shows or hides them (saved in `SpireLedger.conf` as `card_insight`; `card_insight_hotkey` changes the key);
  - they no longer show through the deck view or another screen on top of the shop or a card choice;
  - they no longer follow a picked card flying to your deck;
  - no more stutter the first time they appear (your run history is now read in the background when the run starts) and less work every frame.
- A setting changed before the first fight of a session (like the new I toggle) is now saved.

### Install
Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831558895), or unzip and copy `SpireLedger.dll` and `SpireLedger.json` into the game's `mods` folder.
