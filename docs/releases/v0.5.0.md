### New
- **Builds tab in the Ledger** (Compendium → Ledger → Builds). It reads the game's own run history, so every finished run counts, with or without the mod:
  - your win rate per character, and per relic and card that ended in your build;
  - how often you picked each card when it was offered;
  - filters by character and solo / co-op; sort by most taken or best win rate (3+ runs).
- **Highlights**: your favorite card and relic, and your lucky and cursed ones (best and worst win rate over at least a tenth of your runs).
- **The game's own art**: card portraits, relic and character icons in every list (names on hover).
- **Each run's final build**: the run detail shows your relics as icons and your final deck as small cards (copies, upgrades).

Colours compare an entry with its own column's average: relics and cards pile up in long runs, so they always beat the overall win rate.

### Fixed
- Solo runs now count in the card history under card choices (the game stores the solo player under another id).
