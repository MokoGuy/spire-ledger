### New
- **Card choices** (rewards, "choose a card", shop): under each offered card, how many copies are already in your deck (and upgraded), how many times you played it this run, and its history over all your runs — runs with the card in your final deck and how many you won (from the game's own run history, so runs played without the mod count too), plus plays per run.
- **Co-op party damage**: each player's damage this fight and share of the party, next to their name in the player list (default) or above their character. **N** switches list → above characters → hidden.
- **In-combat keys**: **V** shows/hides the incoming-damage forecast, **B** the damage chart. Choices are saved; keys are configurable.
- **Max HP loss forecast**: enemies with Paper Cuts make you lose max HP for each hit past your block; the forecast now shows it (e.g. "−2 max HP").
- **Version shown** in the Ledger and the run recap, and a **banner in co-op** when your teammate runs another Spire Ledger version.

### Fixed
- Forecast counted attacks the game shows as **0** (e.g. "0x2" after a big Strength loss) with their base damage.
- Forecast ignored **Burns and curses** in hand in non-English games (it read the English card text); it now uses the game's data, with the right block rules.
- Forecast ignored **Disintegration** and treated blockable self-damage (Constrict, Magic Bomb) as unblockable.
- **Debuffs** are counted like the game's Debuffer ("Ensorceleur") badge — one per application — instead of summing stacks.
- The **last fight** of a run (the one on the game-over screen) is now saved in the run history.
