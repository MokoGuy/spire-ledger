### Changed
- **The forecast's toggle key is now F** (it was V, which the game holds for its reaction wheel, so both opened at once). A V saved in your settings switches to F by itself. The other keys (C, B, N, I) were checked against the game's own bindings: no conflict.
- **Your settings now live in `%APPDATA%\SlayTheSpire2\SpireLedger\SpireLedger.conf`**, no longer next to the mod's DLL. With a Workshop subscription the DLL sits in Steam's download folder, which updates can rewrite. Settings saved by an older version next to the DLL are copied over on first launch.

### For mod testers
- A game started without Steam (`--force-steam off`) keeps its settings and run history in `SpireLedger\local`, apart from your real ones.

### Install
Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3831558895), or unzip and copy `SpireLedger.dll` and `SpireLedger.json` into the game's `mods` folder.
